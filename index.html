Geometry and energy definitions

The equilibrium electrochemical potential EF is uniform. The plotted μ(x) = EF − Emid(x) is measured relative to the local midgap; EF − EC = μ − Eg/2.

The gates occupy x < −L/2 and x > L/2, with −d ≤ z ≤ 0. There is no outer gate edge or physical gate-width parameter. The structure is translationally invariant along y.

Self-consistent 2D Poisson–Thomas–Fermi model with sheet charge and quantum capacitance. hBN: εx = 6.9, εz = 3.4; SiO₂: ε = 3.9; air: ε = 1. Representative monolayer WSe₂ band parameters.

D is measured from the metal top to the sheet; H from the metal bottom to Si. The sheet and hBN are flat. Metal continues to the outer numerical boundary, with zero normal flux there to approximate the far field. The extension factor controls numerical truncation, not gate width.

Nonzero μ₀ defines a uniformly doped, compensated reference state. Applied voltage has no fixed input ceiling; convergence and the assumptions of this electrostatic band model still apply. See model notes.
